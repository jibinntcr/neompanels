MANUFACTURING & QUALITY

Our factory. The foundation of our business.

NEOM Panels has its own manufacturing facility in India. It is an important part of the company we are building and the relationship we want customers to have with our materials.

This page brings together approved information about the facility, how manufacturing discussions begin and the supporting information available for business enquiries.

MANUFACTURING WORKFLOW STRUCTURE

From material intake to dispatch accountability.

Illustrative overview of core panel manufacturing stages. Specific process steps depend on the exact product family and grade under discussion.

STAGE 01
Material intake
Incoming raw material inspection and source documentation recording.
STAGE 02
Preparation
Conditioning and material preparation suited to the specified panel construction.
STAGE 03
Panel manufacture
Controlled pressing and board formation within defined operating parameters.
STAGE 04
Finishing
Trimming, calibration and surface preparation to specified dimensions.
STAGE 05
Product checks
Batch-level inspection against the applicable grade and specification.
STAGE 06
Packing & dispatch
Protective bundling, batch identification and dispatch coordination.
