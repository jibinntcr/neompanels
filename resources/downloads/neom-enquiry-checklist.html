Seven Details to Define Before Requesting a Panel Quotation

Use this worksheet to prepare a clear specification before submitting your requirement to NEOM Panels. You do not need every answer before contacting us—identifying open questions early helps our team focus the technical discussion.

1. Intended Application Describe what the panel will become (e.g., modular wardrobe carcass, office workstation surface, interior wall panelling, institutional seating) and the operating conditions.
2. Product Family & Grade Include the brand (e.g., Starwoods), product family or wording from your approved material schedule. If open for recommendation, note “Selection open”.
3. Dimensions & Thickness State panel length, width and thickness with explicit units (mm / ft), plus any critical machining or thickness tolerance requirements.
4. Quantity, Unit & Frequency Specify quantity with unit (sheets, sq. m, etc.) and indicate whether this is a one-time project order or a recurring production requirement.
5. Delivery Destination Provide the city, state and any site unloading or scheduling considerations.
6. Required Documentation List any technical data sheets, test scopes or project compliance documents required for your evaluation.
7. Target Schedule Indicate your project evaluation stage or requested delivery window for discussion.
Confidentiality & Procurement Note: Before attaching a BOQ or schedule to a website enquiry, remove confidential commercial pricing or restricted personal information. Submitting an enquiry does not constitute a formal tender bid, purchase order or confirmation of product eligibility.
