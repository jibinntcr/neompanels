Scope: Corporate & B2B Introduction

Panels for spaces. A company built on values.

NEOM Panels is an Indian panel manufacturing business and the company behind Starwoods. With our own manufacturing facility in India, we bring manufacturing capability, material understanding and accountable business relationships together for businesses that make furniture, shape interiors and deliver projects.

How NEOM Works With Business Buyers

Furniture & OEM Manufacturing Discuss panel requirements for furniture production, modular systems and repeat component manufacturing.
Projects & Commercial Interiors Share your application, BOQ or material schedule, quantities and timeline for interior fit-out projects.
Institutions & Public Procurement Connect regarding specification-led institutional requirements and supporting product documentation.
Distribution & Channel Partnerships Introduce your company, operating territory and market experience to explore a relationship with NEOM and its brands.

Our Brands: Starwoods

NEOM Panels brings its product brands together under one corporate identity. Starwoods is a NEOM Panels brand offering a focused destination to explore product information. For recurring supply, project schedules or partnership discussions, buyers can also submit a requirement directly through NEOM Panels.

Core Values & Responsibility Priorities

  • Accountability: Be clear about what we offer, what we commit to and who is responsible.
  • Material understanding: Encourage informed choices through relevant product information and application discussions.
  • Integrity: Communicate honestly and support claims with appropriate evidence.
  • Respect for resources & people: Consider the materials, energy and safe working practices behind every product, guided by SDG 12 (primary reference) and SDGs 8, 9 and 15 as priorities for developing our approach.

Starting a Conversation

Submit your requirement via the Business Enquiry route on the NEOM Panels website (/enquiry), including your intended application, required specification or grade (if known), panel dimensions, quantity with unit and delivery destination.
